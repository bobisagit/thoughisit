Questions humans ask

Is my money gone if my dog gets outbid?

No — this is the important bit. Every bid stacks on your dog's lifetime total permanently. Being outbid just means someone stacked more. Your total (and your shelter contribution) never disappears.

Can girls be the Goodest Boy?

Obviously. "Goodest Boy" is a title, like "Doctor". All dogs of all genders are eligible and are, in fact, already good.

How do I add my dog?

Tap any "Boost" button, or the big orange button up top. Upload a photo, pick a name, bid your first $1 — you're on the board instantly.

What stops a billionaire ruining it?

Weekly crown snapshots. Even if someone parks $10,000 on #1, a fresh race starts every Sunday for the weekly crown and Hall of Fame — and "Goodest in Breed" titles keep every tier fun.

Is this gambling?

No. There's no chance element and nothing to lose — every dollar buys a permanent, visible contribution to your dog's total, like a fundraising thermometer with extra steps and more tail wagging.
